Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fiora ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and financial data is handled securely. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Fiora mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with our budgeting and expense tracking services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect authentication information (such as your email address, Google, or Apple Sign-In details) to securely identify you across devices.
  • Financial Data Only When you Turn On Sync By Yourself to store your data:: Information you input into the App, including transaction amounts, categories, payment methods, budgets, and notes.
  • Usage & Crash Data: We automatically collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the stability and performance of the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Service: Syncing your budgets and transactions across your devices via secure cloud storage.
  • Feature Processing: Using Apple's native speech recognition to transcribe voice input and secure APIs to extract data from receipt images.
  • App Improvement: Analyzing crash data to fix bugs and monitoring anonymous usage patterns to design better features.
  • Monetization: Displaying rewarded advertisements (for non-premium users) using third-party ad networks.

5. Device Permissions

Fiora requires specific permissions to function properly. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your iOS Settings:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Accessed strictly when you explicitly choose to scan or upload a receipt. Images are processed to extract text and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Accessed only when you hold the voice-input button to dictate a transaction.

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data. Your financial transactions and budgets are stored securely using industry-standard encryption provided by Firebase. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the services. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated financial data at any time. If you choose to delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our active databases.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
